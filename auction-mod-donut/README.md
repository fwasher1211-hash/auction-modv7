# Auction Mod for DonutSMP (Fabric, Minecraft 1.21.11) - CLIENT SIDE ONLY

Only the seller needs this mod. Nothing is installed on the server.

RIGHT SHIFT opens the menu (rebind in Controls -> Auction Mod):
  1 Item  - pick any block, or press "Use held item" for anything else
  2 Price - amount + minimum bid (500k, 2.5m, 1b ...)
  3 Time  - duration, then press START AUCTION (the timer starts right then)

While it runs, a panel at the top of your screen shows the highest bid.
Bidders just do /pay <you> <amount>. A player's bid is the TOTAL they have paid you during the auction.
When the timer ends you are told who won and who needs a refund (nothing is paid out automatically).

Everything is done in the menu - there are no commands to type.
The Test bid button (live view) simulates a payment from "TestPlayer"; the Time tab has a troubleshooting toggle that shows raw payment messages.
